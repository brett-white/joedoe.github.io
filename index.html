The Problem

The "Perk Gap" is killing employee experience

Benefit cards were built for a fixed-budget world. Real life is never exact — and that gap is expensive for everyone.

The Awkward Decline

Benefit cards decline when the bill is as little as $0.01 over budget — causing friction at the register and embarrassing employees in front of colleagues.

Receipt Hell

Employees waste 2+ hours a month tracking small meal receipts. Finance wastes 10+ hours auditing them. A tax on everyone's time.

The Ghost Canteen

Companies pay for expensive catering that 40% of staff don't even want. Budget spent, morale missed, talent lost.

2 hrs / employee / month lost to receipts 10 hrs / month finance overhead 40% canteen spend wasted
The Solution

JoeDoe: the invisible benefit engine

One tap. One clean approval. No friction at the register — ever.

Ring-Fenced Control

Companies allocate funds to specific spend categories (e.g. Daily Lunch) with hard caps. Zero budget overrun, guaranteed.

Never-Decline Engine

Your daily stipend caps what your company pays — never what you can spend. If a purchase runs over, it still goes through in full: one tap, one clean approval.

Zero Merchant Friction

Merchants receive a standard Visa/Mastercard authorization. No contract with us, no new hardware, no awareness of JoeDoe at all.

Zero-Receipt Audit

Every tap is a digital log — merchant, category, amount, timestamp. Finance gets an automated record. No manual claims, ever.
